# Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to AC-Harness. This document covers the first-week checklist for all new hires regardless of department.

## Day 1
- Pick up laptop and badge from IT desk on the 4th floor.
- Sign into SSO via Cognito with the temporary password sent by your manager.
- Verify access to the shared wiki and your department channel.

## Week 1
- Complete security awareness training (mandatory; tracked by HR).
- Schedule a 30-min intro with your buddy.
- Read your department's playbook (linked from the wiki landing page).
