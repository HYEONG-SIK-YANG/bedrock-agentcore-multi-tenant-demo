# Sales Playbook (Public Excerpt)

This is the publicly shared excerpt of the Sales playbook. Confidential pricing and account-specific notes live in the Sales-only space.

## Discovery call structure
1. Confirm the customer's primary problem in their own words.
2. Map their stack to AC-Harness modules (Runtime, Memory, Gateway, Identity, Harness, Registry, Policy, Evaluations).
3. Identify three measurable success criteria.

## Qualification framework
We use MEDDPICC. The shared framework definition is:
- **M**etrics — quantified business impact
- **E**conomic buyer — who signs the PO
- **D**ecision criteria — what they will evaluate against
- **D**ecision process — how the decision flows
- **P**aper process — legal / procurement timing
- **I**dentify pain — current cost of inaction
- **C**hampion — internal advocate
- **C**ompetition — incumbent or alternative under evaluation

## Demo request
Engineering pre-sales staffs demos within 3 business days for qualified opportunities ≥ $50k ARR.
