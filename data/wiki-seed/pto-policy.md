# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

AC-Harness offers 20 days of PTO per calendar year for full-time employees, accrued monthly.

## Accrual
- 1.67 days per month, prorated for hires after the 15th.
- Unused days carry over up to 5 days into the next year; the rest is forfeited on Jan 1.

## Requesting PTO
- Submit through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance for blocks ≥ 3 days.
- Same-day sick leave: notify your manager via Slack before 10:00 local time.

## Public holidays
The company observes 11 public holidays per year. The current calendar is published at the start of each fiscal year.

## Parental leave
Separate from PTO. See parental-leave.md (HR-only).
